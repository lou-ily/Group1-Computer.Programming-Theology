Saint Augustine's Journey


354 — Early Life

Augustine was born in Thagaste in North Africa and developed a strong love for learning.

Family — Monica & Patricius

His mother Monica was a devoted Christian, while his father Patricius followed a different religious path before becoming Christian later.

Search — Searching for Truth

Augustine spent years exploring philosophy, ideas, and different ways of understanding truth.

386 — Conversion

After a long spiritual search, Augustine experienced a profound conversion to Christianity.

395 — Bishop of Hippo

Augustine became bishop of Hippo and devoted himself to teaching, writing, and serving others.

Legacy

His writings became highly influential in Christian thought and philosophy.
